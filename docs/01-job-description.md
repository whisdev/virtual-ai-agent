# Job Description: AI Agent Architecture Consultant

## About the Project

We are building a new AI product for the German market: a **multi-agent system** that operates like a small virtual company. Different AI agents will handle distinct business functions—marketing, SEO, content creation, and lead management—while a central orchestrator coordinates the workflow and communicates with the user.

## What We're Looking For

We need someone who:

- **Understands AI agent architectures** — multi-agent systems, orchestration, tool use, and agentic loops
- **Has hands-on experience with OpenClaw / ClawBot** — production-grade agent design, gateway patterns, session management
- Can discuss **concept, architecture, and technical approach** before we write code

## Your Role (Phase 1)

This starts as a **consultation** to:

1. Review our idea and validate the architecture
2. Discuss the best way to build the system
3. Identify risks, trade-offs, and alternatives
4. Align on technical stack and implementation strategy

If the collaboration works well, there is a possibility of continuing together into the **full development** of the AI agent system.

## Preferred Background

- Experience designing or building multi-agent AI systems
- Familiarity with OpenClaw, ClawBot, or similar production agent architectures
- Understanding of orchestration patterns (central coordinator vs. peer-to-peer)
- Experience with LLM tool-calling, agentic loops, and memory/context management

## Nice to Have

- Experience with the **German-speaking market** (DACH: Germany, Austria, Switzerland)
- Understanding of **European business needs** — compliance, localization, B2B workflows
- Prior work with marketing, SEO, or CRM automation

## Engagement Model

- **Phase 1:** Consultation — review, discuss, advise
- **Phase 2 (optional):** Full development — design and build the system together

## How to Apply

Please reach out with:

- A short overview of your experience with AI agents and OpenClaw/ClawBot
- Links to relevant projects or publications
- Your availability for an initial consultation call
